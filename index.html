Ten small apps, each keeping one honest record.

KSQ Studios is a one-person studio in Berwyn, Pennsylvania, building apps for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Each app has a palette of five colors, and in eight of them the color is the reading. Here they are, bound together like a fan deck.

Most recent release: Brightened 1.2, November 2026.

Ten palettes, one studio.

The apps

Minuted

Every meeting has a price tag.

A meeting cost calculator that shows what's on the line, in dollars, as it happens. Pick a roster, hit start, watch the room burn money in real time.

minuted.orgApp Store

Winnowed

Keep what counts. Cut the rest.

A subscription tracker built around a quarterly review ritual. Sit with your subscriptions four times a year, see their true 5-year cost, and decide what's worth keeping.

winnowed.appApp Store

Endured

Hard work, honestly recorded.

A focus session recorder built around a simple contract: the record is what happened. The gradient deepens as you work, from bone through stone, slate and graphite to iron, and sessions are saved exactly as long as they were.

endured.appApp Store

Eclipsed

Sleep debt, made visible.

A sleep debt tracker that reads HealthKit, computes a rolling total, and surfaces it through a five-stage gradient. The color warms with the weight you're carrying: slate when rested, crimson when you've been short for weeks.

eclipsedsleep.appApp Store

Vesperine

Write once, at the end of the day.

A contemplative evening journal. Write once when the day has settled, and the Mirror offers a single reflection in return, generated on your device. No streaks, no scores, nothing that nudges or scolds.

vesperine.appApp Store

Brightened

The daylight you gathered.

A daylight tracker built on the Time in Daylight your Apple Watch already records. A five-stage gradient brightens as light accumulates, from slate through dawn to radiant, and nothing ever scolds you for a quiet week.

brightened.appApp Store

Reckonry

Record what you reckon before you start.

An estimate keeper. Say how long you think a task will take, do the work, and see the gap between the two. A five-stage arc from teal to magenta carries the size of the miss, and there is no score, no streak, and no coaching anywhere in it.

reckonry.appApp Store

Commuted

Your commute costs hours before it costs dollars.

A commute cost tracker. Tap when you leave, tap when you arrive, and see what the trip to work actually takes from you. The hours lead; the money follows. It uses the IRS standard mileage rate rather than fuel alone, and asks for no permissions at all.

commuted.appApp Store

Adjourned

End the meetings you should not be in.

A standing meeting review built around a monthly ritual. Every recurring meeting priced in hours and dollars, one question per card, then a decision: keep, shorten, or cut. It never cancels anything for you, and the number only falls when the meeting actually ends.

adjournedapp.comApp Store

Moratine

Time you did not choose.

A record of involuntary waiting: on hold, in line, in waiting rooms. One tap starts it and one tap ends it, from Control Center, your watch, or a widget. Say who you waited on later, or never, and the named ones add up into a ledger of who is costing you time.

moratine.appApp Store

About the studio

KSQ Studios is the work of Kurt Klein, a software engineer who, like most people who write code for a living, eventually wanted to make something small and his own.

  • Good software should respect your time and attention.
  • Costs should be visible rather than hidden.
  • One-time purchases are the right shape for tools you keep on your home screen for years.

Privacy is not a footnote. None of the apps require an account. None of them sync your data anywhere they do not need to. What lives on your device stays on your device.
