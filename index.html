KSQ Studios is a one-person studio in Pennsylvania building apps for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Quiet design. Honest pricing. No subscriptions for things that should be one-time purchases.

What we make

Minuted

Every meeting has a price tag.

A meeting cost calculator that shows what's on the line, in dollars, as it happens. Pick a roster, hit start, watch the room burn money in real time.

minuted.org

Winnowed

Keep what counts. Cut the rest.

A subscription tracker built around a quarterly review ritual. Sit with your subscriptions four times a year, see their true 5-year cost, and decide what's worth keeping.

winnowed.app

Endured

Hard work, honestly recorded.

A focus session recorder built around a simple contract: the record is what happened. The gradient deepens as you work — bone, stone, slate, graphite, iron — and sessions are saved exactly as long as they were.

endured.app

Eclipsed

Sleep debt, made visible.

A sleep debt tracker that reads HealthKit, computes a rolling total, and surfaces it through a five-stage gradient. The color warms with the weight you're carrying — slate when rested, crimson when you've been short for weeks.

eclipsedsleep.com

Vesperine

Write once, at the end of the day.

A contemplative evening journal. Write once when the day has settled, and the Mirror offers a single reflection in return, generated on your device. No streaks, no scores, nothing that nudges or scolds.

vesperine.app

About the studio

KSQ Studios is the work of Kurt Klein — a software engineer who, like most people who write code for a living, eventually wanted to make something small and his own.

Every app from KSQ is built around the same handful of beliefs: that good software should respect your time and attention, that costs should be visible rather than hidden, and that one‑time purchases are the right shape for tools you keep on your home screen for years.

Privacy isn’t a footnote. None of our apps require an account. None of them sync your data anywhere we don’t need it to. What lives on your device stays on your device.
